## What we learned

**From the literature review** (claims map in `literature/claims.md`; citation
details unverified against primary sources until each is opened):

- The hurdle loss's presence and magnitude terms factorize exactly (Cragg
  1971; Mullahy 1986), so the weight between them is not a likelihood
  parameter -- it acts only through the shared encoder, and weight-one is the
  only value where `exp(-loss)` is a normalized joint density (Bissiri, Holmes
  & Walker 2016 supplies the correct framing for other values; never write
  "improper scoring rule").
- A hidden averaging mismatch makes the effective weight **21x the nominal
  `magnitude_weight`**: lambda = 1 sits at 0.048; the regen baseline ran
  lambda ~5.3 and trial 22 ran lambda ~163.
- Under misspecification the literature tempers the suspect factor **down**,
  to [0, 1] (Grunwald & van Ommen 2017; semi-modular inference). Our Dirichlet
  is the suspect factor (measured amphibole bimodality), and the sweep moved
  it up 163x.
- Selecting on validation NLL reliably prefers overconfident models even
  in-distribution (Mukhoti et al. 2020) -- the mechanism by which trial 22 won.

**From the real-rock work:**

- The transfer failure is confidence, not knowledge: accuracy and ranking flat
  across versions while coverage collapses (30-39% at nominal 80%). Two named
  qualifications: amphibole is a discrimination failure (AUROC 0.539), and
  accessory minerals drift in log-ratio geometry.
- Paired on the same rocks, every metric agrees on ordering; NLL and CRPS
  disagree only on magnitude, for formal reasons (log score local and
  hypersensitive; CRPS under-sensitive to concentration, Bolin & Wallin 2023).
  Multi-arm comparisons must be paired, unit-clustered contrasts, never
  marginal error bars.
- A ~121-parameter constant correction absorbs almost all of the real-rock
  penalty for every checkpoint. What it cannot absorb: residuals
  heavier-tailed than any Beta at the fitted mean (no widening reaches honest
  coverage without vacuity; post-fit coverage 0.45-0.75), and per-mineral
  biases that trend with silica (the one supported parametric extension:
  an SiO2 term on the magnitude side).

**From the three-way comparison** (identical protocol: matrix tier fit on the
368-rock train roster):

- Sealed test joint NLL: **0.478 / 0.769 / 1.371** for supportmag / regen /
  regen-tuned; all pairwise unit-clustered contrasts exclude zero. Val alone
  could not separate old-vs-regen (-0.33 [-0.76, +0.05]) -- the test slice
  earned its keep.
- Few-shot curves on val: the old-corpus model wins at every labelled-rock
  budget (25 rocks: 3.86 / 5.97 / 6.84 median joint NLL); no crossover.
- Interpretation (mechanism hypothesized, not isolated -- the regen changed
  sampler, draw count, and bulk roster together): trace-conditional draws
  narrowed per-bulk label diversity, deleting rock-dependent hedging the
  broad corpus provided implicitly. Constant recalibration cannot restore
  per-rock width. The traced corpus's real wins: quartz presence (+49 -> +28
  points over-call) and assemblage ranking (median rank 11 -> 8 of 128).
- Three independent lines now point at the same missing structure --
  rock-dependent width: measured amp bimodality, the heavier-than-Beta
  residual, and the value of broad-draw marginalization. All strengthen the
  eventual ModeHead mixture case.
- A lesson about instruments: corpus-side metrics (validation NLL, the #27
  scaling curve) and real-rock transfer can move in opposite directions.
  The real-rock benchmark is the only instrument that survives era churn;
  corpus metrics are advisory diagnostics.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-08-03)

1. **Production stays `joint_heads_supportmag_seed0`** (old corpus, era
   v3.3-sim-to-real). The provisional 2026-07-29 adoption of trial 22 is
   reversed: it lost to its own baseline on every paired real-rock metric, and
   both regen checkpoints lose to the old-corpus model after identical
   correction. No v3.4 tag is rushed; if one is cut it records a finding, not
   a production upgrade. Historical tags are not renamed
   (`v3.2-label-corpus-limited` records a corpus-side lesson that remains true
   on its own metrics).
2. **Cascade 2 trains on the old broad-draw corpus**, not the traced one.
   Baseline-first: retrain heads at current production dials with only the
   all-rock encoder swapped in, so the comparison to production isolates the
   encoder's effect; then the sweep, with the corrected selection criterion
   (report and search effective lambda, range at or below 1; sampled-support
   NLL in the tiebreak). Judged on the real-rock benchmark.
3. **ModeHead Dirichlet mixture** is attempted after cascade 2, on the old
   corpus with whichever encoder wins -- kept if better, dropped if not.
4. **Recalibrator upgrades wait on the mixture verdict** (a working mixture
   changes what the recalibrator needs to be). Always-needed parts regardless:
   constant sim-to-real offsets, and the SiO2 magnitude-side term.

Standing: the corrected-criterion pattern rides every future sweep. The prose
rule stands -- every paper claim grounded in literature, measured findings, or
owner-posited hypotheses, and the climatology null stays internal-only, never
in paper-bound material. #60 (corpus scaling re-measured on the real-rock
axis) is filed, blocked on cascade 2; its curve decides whether a larger
broad-draw regen is generated.
